RESORT 360 OPERATIONAL INTELLIGENCE HANDBOOK
STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES & MULTI-AGENT GOVERNANCE
Document Version: 2.4-DOM
Domain: Luxury Resort & Hospitality Operations

SECTION 1: OVERVIEW AND MISSION
Resort 360 operates as an automated, multi-agent hospitality operations platform. Real-time telemetry, guest requests, maintenance anomalies, and housekeeping readiness feed an AI Context Builder. The domain intelligence layer synthesizes operational incidents to prevent guest dissatisfaction, eliminate inter-departmental conflicts, and protect resort revenue. Under no circumstances may AI autonomous actions modify database state, reassign rooms, or dispatch work orders without explicit human manager authorization.

SECTION 2: GUEST TIERS & VIP PROTOCOLS
1. Tier 1 (VIP Presidential / Ultra High Net Worth):
   - Mandatory escort to Private Club Lounge immediately upon arrival if assigned suite is not inspected and ready.
   - Complementary beverage service and personal welcome from Assistant General Manager or Guest Relations Manager.
   - In case of room maintenance defects (e.g. AC failure, plumbing leak), immediate reassignment to equal or higher tier suite (e.g. Executive Suite or Deluxe Suite) is required within 15 minutes.
   - Max allowable lobby wait time: 0 minutes. Max room readiness delay: 25 minutes.

2. Tier 2 (VIP Regular / Frequent Guest):
   - Priority check-in, express luggage handling.
   - In case of room issue, reassign to comparable room immediately. If not ready, provide F&B credit (INR 4,000) and lounge access.

3. Tier 3 (Standard Leisure / Direct / OTA):
   - Standard check-in time: 15:00 IST. Early arrival before 13:00 accommodated based on clean inventory.
   - If clean inventory unavailable, store luggage, grant pool and dining access.

SECTION 3: HVAC & ENGINEERING FAILURE PROTOCOL
1. Suite AC Compressor Failure:
   - Priority: CRITICAL.
   - Technician dispatch SLA: 5 minutes.
   - Diagnosis SLA: 10 minutes.
   - If diagnosis indicates repair time > 30 minutes and guest is in residence or arriving within 60 minutes:
     a) Front Desk must reassign guest to alternative inspected room.
     b) Housekeeping must perform priority refresh on target reassigned room.
     c) Room with failed AC must be shifted to 'maintenance' status and taken out of active inventory.
     d) Revenue management must decrement available room count from PMS and OTA channels to prevent overbooking.

2. Plumbing & Water Leakage Incidents:
   - Severity: CRITICAL if active drip or pipe burst; HIGH if slow drainage.
   - Action: Water isolation valve shutoff within 5 minutes. Floor drain inspection.
   - Housekeeping dry-vacuum and sanitized towel deployment.

SECTION 4: HOUSEKEEPING TURNOVER & BOTTLENECK ARBITRATION
1. Room Cleaning Priorities:
   - Priority 1: VIP early arrival alternative rooms (Express turnover: 20-25 minutes).
   - Priority 2: Occupied room service requests.
   - Priority 3: Regular checkout turnover for incoming arrivals.
   - Priority 4: Stayover refreshes.
2. Staffing Contention:
   - If housekeeping availability is <= 2 attendants during peak checkout (11:00-14:00):
     Manager must authorize express turn protocols (focus on sanitization, linen swap, bathroom deep clean; postpone carpet shampooing).

SECTION 5: REVENUE & INVENTORY PROTECTION
1. Occupancy Threshold Rules:
   - Low Occupancy (< 60%): High flexibility for complimentary upgrades and early check-ins.
   - Moderate Occupancy (60% - 85%): Upgrades restricted to 1 category tier. Reassigned rooms must be protected from general inventory pool.
   - High Occupancy (> 85%): No complimentary double-upgrades. Every room reassignment must be cross-checked against incoming reservations to avoid displaced guests.

SECTION 6: ESCALATION & HUMAN APPROVAL THRESHOLDS
Manager Approval is strictly required when:
1. Reassignment involves upgrading a guest more than 1 category tier.
2. Estimated maintenance repair cost exceeds INR 10,000 or repair window exceeds 2 hours.
3. Hotel occupancy exceeds 85% and a room must be taken out of order.
4. Guest compensation exceeds INR 5,000 F&B credit.
5. Multiple concurrent critical incidents compete for a single available technician.
